How to use the War Room

Install it on your phone

iPhone (needs iOS 16.4 or newer): open the site in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Open it from the new icon from now on.

Android: open the site in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".

Get alerts

Tap Alerts, choose what you want, tap Turn on, and say Allow when the phone asks. On iPhone this only works from the Home Screen icon, not from a Safari tab.

Nothing arrives? iPhone: Settings, Notifications, War Room, Allow Notifications. Android: Settings, Apps, Chrome (or the app), Notifications. Focus and Do Not Disturb modes silence alerts. Then turn alerts off and on again in the app.

Share a spy or combat report

In OGame, open the report and copy its report ID (it starts with sr- or cr-). Paste it into the report box on the Players screen (a whole report page works too). Everyone then sees that player's tech, and flight times use it.

Players and planets

Players shows everyone we have a report on. The DONUT and PMA tabs list those alliances. The box under them swaps the PMA tab for any alliance. Tap a player for their tech and planets.

The line on each card shows planets left to right, galaxy 1 to 7. A blue dot is a moon. The star is the capital (our guess: the oldest planet).

The map

Map shows the whole universe: the 7 galaxies sit in a ring (galaxy 7 is next to galaxy 1). Tap a galaxy, or a number, to open it. In a galaxy the systems run around the ring, and a planet's number is how far out it sits (1 inside, 15 outside). Orange is us, red is the second alliance (change it with the alliance box on Players), moons are the small light dots. Tap a planet to see who owns it. Arrows are flights with a known launch planet, in the flight colours.

Tap a planet that has a moon to record a jump gate and its phalanx level. Gates get a green ring, and the gates of one player are joined by a dotted line. A phalanx level shows a green wedge of the systems it can scan (level squared minus 1, each way), plus a list of the enemy planets inside it. Everyone sees what anyone saves. A pasted spy report on a moon fills these in by itself (when the probes could see its buildings).

Log a flight (landings)

On Targets, fill in the form, or type one sentence like "enemy attack 1 98 5 from 3 12 4, 3 million, lands in 2 hours 15 minutes, back in 4 hours" and tap "Fill in the form from this". Mission words it knows: attack, ACS attack, ACS defend, moon destruction, transport, deploy, spy, harvest, expedition, colonize, return (say "returning to 1 159 7 in 2 hours").

Every flight gets a colour: Alert (red) is an attack on us that can be defended. Opportunity (gold) is an enemy fleet that will be at a known place at a known time, such as an enemy fleet arriving, or back home. Info (blue) is everything else. You can change it under "More".

Alerts go to everyone who turned them on. Enemy flights that are only info stay quiet.
